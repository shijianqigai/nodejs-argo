Why We Need To Protect Nature

The Earth is the only home for all living creatures. Forests, oceans, rivers and wildlife maintain the balance of the ecosystem. Pollution, deforestation and global warming are destroying our living environment day by day. It is everyone’s responsibility to guard the planet and leave a clean world for future generations.

Protect Forests

Forests are the lungs of the earth. They absorb carbon dioxide, produce oxygen and provide habitats for thousands of wild animals. Stop illegal logging and plant more trees to restore green coverage.

Guard Ocean Resources

Marine plastic pollution kills countless sea animals every year. Reduce disposable plastic products, sort garbage and avoid dumping waste into rivers and seas.

Fight Global Warming

Excessive carbon emissions lead to rising temperatures, melting glaciers and extreme weather. Choose low-carbon travel, save electricity and cut energy waste in daily life.

Main Threats To Our Environment

White Plastic Pollution

Disposable plastic bags, tableware and bottles cannot degrade in hundreds of years. They contaminate soil, water and enter the food chain, harming human health.

Air & Water Pollution

Industrial waste gas, vehicle exhaust and unprocessed sewage damage air quality and freshwater resources, triggering respiratory diseases and water shortage crises.

Biodiversity Loss

Habitat destruction and overhunting cause many species to go extinct. Once the biological chain breaks, human survival will face huge risks.

Simple Green Habits Everyone Can Keep

  • Sort household waste and recycle paper, metal and plastic products
  • Use reusable shopping bags, water cups and chopsticks instead of disposable items
  • Turn off lights and electrical equipment when not in use to save energy
  • Walk, ride bikes or take public transport to reduce car carbon emissions
  • Save tap water; reuse water for watering flowers and cleaning floors
  • Plant trees, flowers and green plants at home or in the community
  • Refuse wild animal products and protect wild species
  • Spread environmental knowledge to family, friends and classmates
